JobKit Autofill privacy policy

Publisher: Ravi Kumar. This extension is a personal job-application helper. It never clicks Submit on a job form.

Data stored on your device

Name, email, phone, resume-related answers, and custom fields you type are saved in this browser’s extension storage (chrome.storage.local). That data stays on your computer unless you export it.

Data sent over the network

  • Optional AI: unmatched form question labels plus the facts already in your vault may be sent to a local host at 127.0.0.1, which may call Anthropic, OpenAI, or Google using your API key. The publisher does not receive that traffic.
  • No analytics, ads, or third-party trackers are included.

What we do not collect

The publisher does not operate a cloud account for this extension. SMTP passwords, payment cards, and resume files are not uploaded by the extension. File inputs cannot be filled; you attach a resume yourself.

Payments

If the listing is paid, checkout is only through Microsoft Edge Add-ons or the Chrome Web Store on the publisher’s developer account. There is no in-extension store, affiliate link, or shared billing.

Contact

Questions: the email on the store listing (the publisher’s account).
